# Nibash automated inbox

Approved scope: authenticated, private inbox; bell with unread count; all/unread filters;
read/unread and mark-all-read; paginated history; links to the relevant contract.

Events: monthly payment created (tenant), payment confirmed/rejected (tenant),
contract proposed (tenant), signed (owner), completed (both), and expiring (both).
Expiry starts one calendar month before end_date, clamped at month end. Dates and
scheduled checks use Asia/Dhaka. Backfill existing pending/overdue payments and
signed contracts already in the expiry window, but no historical completed events.
