# Rental Protection & Records Policy

Version 1.0 · Issued 3 November 2026

Effective upon publication of this version on Nibash. It applies to account registrations and new rental agreements accepted after publication, as described below.

Nibash provides tools for residential renting in Bangladesh. This policy explains the standards landlords and tenants agree to follow when using its rental, agreement, payment-record and tenant-management services. Read it before registering and before accepting a rental agreement. Keep a copy for your records.

## 1. Scope, acceptance and applicable law

By selecting the policy agreement checkbox when registering, you agree to follow this policy when using the services it covers. Registration does not accept a particular property, rent revision or rental agreement on your behalf. Each such agreement or revision requires its own review and acceptance.

This policy sets Nibash's platform rules. It is not a complete statement of Bangladesh law, an individual lease, a general Privacy Policy or a substitute for professional advice. Mandatory law and binding orders take priority. Nothing here waives a person's statutory rights, makes an unlawful charge lawful, or prevents a person from seeking a remedy from a competent authority or court.

Existing records are not retrospectively certified by this policy. Where the true tenancy start or rent history is uncertain, Nibash will identify that uncertainty rather than invent a protection history. Parties must not use an unsupported or unavailable platform feature to misrepresent their legal rights or obligations.

## 2. Accurate information and responsible use

Provide accurate registration and rental information, protect your account, and review documents before accepting them. A landlord or manager must be authorized to manage the property and must disclose the agreed recurring charges. A tenant must accurately describe payments made and notify the landlord of errors in the rental record.

Do not submit false payment claims, confirm money you have not received, alter evidence, impersonate another party or misuse information obtained through a rental relationship. Do not disguise a prohibited deposit or premium as a service charge, membership payment, reservation charge or another fee. The name of a charge does not determine whether it is permitted.

## 3. Nibash's 24-month rent protection

Nibash contractual protection: for a new residential agreement accepted through Nibash under this policy, the initial effective monthly rent is protected against ordinary increases for 24 calendar months from the recorded beginning of the continuing tenancy. The rent-effective tenancy start, rather than the day of account registration or agreement acceptance, starts this period.

For example, rent of BDT 20,000 effective from 15 November 2026 becomes eligible for an ordinary increase no earlier than 15 November 2028. Eligibility does not itself increase the rent or oblige the tenant to accept a proposal.

A shorter contract, extension, continuing renewal, duplicate agreement or change of landlord or manager must not reset or bypass the original protection. A rent decrease may be agreed without erasing the original rent and protection history. Increasing mandatory recurring charges to avoid the protection is not permitted. A genuinely new tenancy following confirmed vacancy may have a new baseline; relabeling a continuing tenancy does not create one.

This is Nibash's own contractual protection, not a claim that every residential rent increase in Bangladesh is prohibited by statute for the same period. [Section 16(2) of the Premises Rent Control Act, 1991](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-748/section-30646.html) concerns the Controller's redetermination of standard rent. Any binding order or case requiring different legal treatment must be referred for human review; an ordinary platform proposal must not be used to bypass that review.

## 4. Rent revisions and agreement changes

A landlord proposing an ordinary rent revision must state the amount and the proposed effective date. Nibash checks the recorded protection period. An increase cannot take effect before the eligible date, and the tenant must separately accept an ordinary negotiated revision before it applies through Nibash. A tenant may decline a proposal.

An accepted revision preserves the previous agreement and records the revised amount, effective date and acceptance. Neither party may silently rewrite accepted terms. Future invoices use the accepted effective rent applicable when they are generated; previously issued invoices and payment acknowledgments retain their recorded amounts. The current monthly-invoice workflow does not automatically prorate midmonth changes.

Rent, utility obligations and recurring service charges must be stated separately. An agreement must not contain an undisclosed compulsory payment or a clause intended to defeat this policy. Changes outside the supported revision workflow require separate review; they are not approved merely because a party enters them in a message or document.

## 5. Advance rent, security deposits and additional charges

[Section 10 of the Premises Rent Control Act, 1991](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-748/section-30640.html) addresses premiums, salami, security and similar additional payments, and separately requires prior Controller approval for advance rent exceeding one month. Advance rent and a security deposit are distinct.

Nibash's ordinary residential workflow sets new security deposits to BDT 0 and does not support their creation or collection. Ordinary advance rent must not exceed one month's monthly rent. For monthly rent of BDT 20,000, the ordinary advance limit is BDT 20,000. A request for BDT 60,000 is rejected in that workflow.

A landlord cannot self-approve an exception or treat an uploaded document as automatic regulatory approval. [Section 11](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-748/section-30641.html) contains a specific development-lease exception; statutory exceptions and approval-dependent collection are outside this ordinary workflow and require separate legal review.

Confirmed advance is recorded separately and credited against rent obligations through the payment records. It must not be counted again as a second cash receipt when used as credit. Historical deposit records remain visible for accounting or disputes; their presence does not approve new collection, establish their lawfulness or convert them into advance rent. Unused advance and disputed balances must be addressed in the parties' settlement record; Nibash does not automatically execute a refund.

## 6. Payments and electronic acknowledgments

Recording a payment is different from confirming receipt. For cash, the tenant records the amount actually paid and the landlord must confirm the specific amount received. A pending cash claim does not generate a paid acknowledgment. A partial confirmed payment produces an acknowledgment for that installment, with the remaining invoice balance where applicable.

Nibash records authenticated party declarations. Unless a payment is expressly processed through an identified official provider integration, a recorded bank or mobile-money method does not mean Nibash independently verified settlement. No person may describe a pending claim as a confirmed payment.

Confirmed events receive an electronic payment acknowledgment with party and property details, payment references, amount, method, timestamps and an integrity reference. The original record is retained. A reversal or correction is linked to it rather than silently replacing or deleting it. Reporting dates are presented in Bangladesh time where indicated.

[Section 13](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-748/section-30643.html) requires the landlord to promptly provide a signed receipt in the prescribed form and retain its counterfoil. Nibash's electronic payment acknowledgment is not represented as a legally certified substitute. Landlords remain responsible for any additional statutory form or signature requirements.

## 7. Agreements, acceptance and registration

A proposed agreement is a DRAFT until the applicable acceptance workflow is completed. An accepted PDF records the captured terms and authenticated account actions; it does not contain an invented handwritten signature. Accepted versions and amendments are preserved for the parties' records.

[Section 107 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-48/section-print-16322.html) requires registered instruments for specified leases, including terms exceeding one year, year-to-year leases and leases reserving yearly rent. Parties must obtain appropriate execution and registration advice. A downloadable PDF or account checkbox does not by itself establish a registered lease or a legally recognized digital signature.

## 8. Expiry, move-out and settlement

A contract end date alone does not establish that a tenant has physically left, that an inspection is complete or that all balances are settled. Nibash treats expiry, occupancy, move-out, inspection, settlement and final closure as separate matters. Its ordinary closure workflow requires matching confirmations from both parties and retains the agreement and financial history.

[Section 18 of the Premises Rent Control Act](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-748/section-30648.html) provides possession-related safeguards subject to conditions and exceptions. Neither this policy nor a platform status authorizes unlawful eviction, lockout, threats or interference with services. Disputed possession and legal entitlement must be resolved through the appropriate lawful process.

## 9. Tenant information and DMP preparation

Landlord tenant-management access is limited to authorized rental relationships. Use contact and tenancy information only for legitimate rental administration and related lawful purposes. Ordinary tenant lists must not expose complete NID or passport numbers. Do not upload identity-document images or sensitive household details merely to fill this dashboard, and do not publish another person's tenant records.

DMP preparation tools help organize existing authorized information and self-reported tracking. Available labels include Not prepared, Prepared and Submitted manually — unverified. Saving one of these labels does not send information to police, establish registration or prove police approval. Consult the [official DMP tenant registration form](https://dmp.gov.bd/important-forms/tenant-registration-form/) and [CIMS information](https://dmp.gov.bd/citizen-info-management-system/) for the current official process.

Sensitive document collection and export are outside this preparation workflow. A tenant may review their own information and request correction through the rental record or the support contact shown on Nibash. Describe the correction without sending unnecessary identity documents or complete identity numbers. A tenant's agreement to this policy does not by itself establish every legal basis for collecting or disclosing personal information.

## 10. Access, integrity and records

Agreement documents and payment acknowledgments are provided through authenticated access for the parties and authorized managers. Do not share account credentials or redistribute private records without a legitimate basis. An integrity hash helps identify changed bytes; it does not independently certify identity, legal validity or government approval.

Rental-policy enforcement and financial calculations do not require AI. Tenant-management identity information and complete personal agreements must not be sent to an AI service merely to enforce this policy. Any separate optional AI feature requires its own appropriate notice and safeguards.

Original financial and agreement records must be preserved for accounting, disputes and applicable legal retention requirements. Correction requests must retain an audit trail rather than silently alter historical transactions. This policy does not promise indefinite retention regardless of law, or prevent legally required access, restriction, preservation or deletion. Broader account-data practices require a separate privacy notice.

## 11. Questions, disputes and legal-source status

For a platform issue or suspected policy breach, use the support contact displayed on Nibash. Identify the agreement or payment reference, explain the issue and provide only necessary evidence. Nibash's records and assistance do not determine every dispute or guarantee payment recovery, a refund or a particular legal outcome. Users retain access to competent authorities, the Rent Controller where applicable, courts and professional advice.

The [Premises Rent Control Act, 1991](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-748.html), including its standard-rent framework in Sections 15–16, and the Transfer of Property Act are statutory sources. The linked official texts, applicable amendments and binding decisions govern legal interpretation; this English policy is not an official translation of legislation.

[DNCC's published policy collection](https://dncc.gov.bd/pages/policies) includes local rental guidance. Its current text, scope and subsequent clarifications must be checked when relevant. Nibash does not treat DNCC announcements as an independent nationwide law or impose a nationwide June–July-only rent-increase window on that basis. DMP information is official process guidance; it is not proof that Nibash has a police integration.

## 12. Policy versions and existing agreements

Nibash will identify published policy versions and their effective status. A future version must not be used to erase the protection history of an existing accepted agreement or silently rewrite that agreement. Material agreement changes require the applicable review and acceptance process, subject to mandatory law and binding orders.

Keep the policy version and agreement records relevant to your rental. If a record is incomplete, a source is uncertain or an exceptional legal situation arises, request review rather than supplying invented dates, approvals or verification claims.
